overheard

Self-hosted voice rooms that remember. The sky above the port is the color of a dead channel; every construct on this grid tapes every channel, and when the last operator drops the line, a daemon writes down everything that was said — who said it, when, and who was talking over whom.

what it is

  • browser voice rooms — WebRTC/Opus through a self-hosted mediasoup SFU; no vendor, no app.
  • per-speaker tapes — every participant recorded as a separate identified Ogg/Opus track, RTP-clock-anchored to a shared room timeline.
  • honest transcripts — offline whisper per track, merged with real overlap detection; simultaneous speech is marked, never flattened into false turn-taking.
  • stage directions — mutes, deafens, joins and leaves land in the transcript where they actually happened.
  • radical transparency — no accounts, no private rooms: everyone on an instance sees and hears everything. Not a Zoom. New rules.

source on github security posture

the verb ladder

  • jack in / jack out — enter the grid, or burn your handle.
  • patch in / drop the line — join a construct (under your handle or any alias), or leave it.
  • silence your mic · go deaf — cut what you send, or what you hear. The tape notes both.
  • flatline — when the last operator leaves, the construct seals forever and wintermute parses the tapes into cold storage.

jack in — what happens on the wire
